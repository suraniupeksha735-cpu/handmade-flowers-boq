## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an intimate, artisanal sanctuary celebrating handcrafted floral artistry. Tailored for discerning gift-givers, interior enthusiasts, and couples seeking everlasting botanical keepsakes, the aesthetic bridges delicate nostalgia with contemporary boutique luxury.

The style unites **Soft Glassmorphism** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- Translucent, milk-frosted layers that evoke vellum wraps and glass greenhouse panels.
- A delicate, airy structural composition layered with organic warmth, steering clear of stark commercialism.
- Tactile, pill-shaped interactions evoking the soft bends and velvet pile of twisted pipe cleaner petals.
- Evoked sentiments: tender reverence, poetic delight, enduring charm, and bespoke craftsmanship.

## Colors
The palette balances vibrant pastel hues with a commanding, velvety floral anchor on a warm parchment foundation.

- **Primary (`#BE123C` Deep Velvet Rose):** Grounding anchor for primary calls to action, high-emphasis interactive states, brand iconography, and key focal points.
- **Secondary (`#EC4899` Petal Blush):** Romantic expressive tone for highlights, badges, active tabs, and secondary interactive moments.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` Lavender Lilac):** Whispering botanical accent used for festive tags, bespoke collection markers, and subtle decorative gradient undertones.
- **Neutral (`#FFFDFB` Soft Cream Canvas):** The organic default background resembling handmade cotton paper. Neutral darks are tinted with rich plum undertones (`#3F1D28` for high-contrast text, `#785966` for secondary copy) rather than sterile carbon blacks.
- **Atmospheric Accents (Functional Pastels):** 
  - Soft Blush (`#FCE7F3`): Romantic containers, subtle card backdrops.
  - Lavender Wash (`#EDE9FE`): Crafting detail cards, material tags.
  - Butter Yellow / Marigold (`#FEF3C7` / `#F59E0B`): Warm care instructions, seasonal stamps, star ratings.
  - Mint Sage (`#D1FAE5` / `#10B981`): Stem foliage accents, freshness indicators, in-stock notices.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy creates an editorial dialogue between traditional artisanal grace and crisp contemporary legibility.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in **Playfair Display**, evoking boutique floral catalogues, bespoke monogramming, and high-fashion editorial covers. Italics are reserved for poetic emphasis, collection quotes, or provenance labels (e.g., *Handmade with care*).
- **Body & Captions:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering open counters and rounded terminals that soften technical readability without looking playful or juvenile.
- **Rhythm & Proportions:** Maintain comfortable line heights to preserve breathing room across editorial storytelling cards and custom order builder workflows.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-grid structure prioritizing negative space to mimic the unhurried elegance of an art gallery.

- **Grid Systems:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas constrained to `1280px` centered.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Atmospheric Padding:** Elements require deliberate white space; product photographs and storytelling blocks must avoid cramped edges. Use `space-lg` and `space-xl` for interior padding within cards and modal surfaces.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons dark industrial drop shadows in favor of luminous, layered translucent surfaces and rose-tinted ambient diffusion.

- **Surface Tiers & Frosted Glass:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Solid `#FFFDFB`.
  - **Tier 1 (Cards, Modules):** Semi-opaque cream with frosted backing (`rgba(255, 253, 251, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
  - **Tier 2 (Popovers, Sticky Navigation, Modals):** Ultra-smooth frosted blush (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Ambient Rose Shadows:**
  - **Soft (Resting Cards):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(190, 18, 60, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(120, 89, 102, 0.03)`
  - **Hover (Interactive Elements):** `0 16px 32px -6px rgba(190, 18, 60, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(120, 89, 102, 0.05)`
  - **Elevated (Modals, Overlays):** `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(190, 18, 60, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(63, 29, 40, 0.06)`
- **Delicate Outlines:** Every glassmorphic panel features a microscopic hairline border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` on the top/left edge and `1px solid rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.12)` along the bottom/right.

## Shapes
The shape language mirrors the gentle, looping bends of wired chenille stems and sculpted botanical petals.

- **Extreme Softness (`roundedness: 3`):** Interactive primitives—including buttons, input chips, pill badges, and quantity pickers—utilize full continuous pill shapes (`9999px` radius).
- **Panel Curvature:** Feature cards, image carousels, and dialog boxes receive generous corner treatment (`rounded-lg` at `2rem`, `rounded-xl` at `3rem`).
- **Organic Inner Offsets:** Media wrappers and inset image holders echo outer container curvature with nested concentric radiuses.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Velvet rose (`#BE123C`) fill with pure white label. Continuous pill shape. Subtle inner glow (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). On hover: scales smoothly to `1.02` with amplified ambient rose shadow.
- **Secondary:** Frosted petal blush (`#FCE7F3`) with velvet rose (`#BE123C`) label and border `1px solid rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.25)`.
- **Tertiary (Ghost):** Transparent body with `#BE123C` text, gaining an ethereal lavender tint (`#EDE9FE`) on hover.

### Chips & Tags
- **Selection Chips:** Pill-shaped, compact padding (`0.375rem 0.875rem`). Resting state utilizes subtle cream translucency; active state turns solid velvet rose or pastel mint depending on category context, with smooth transition timings.
- **Botanical Badges:** Pastel pods (Lavender `#EDE9FE`, Mint `#D1FAE5`, Butter `#FEF3C7`) with contrasting deep-toned text (`label-sm`, uppercase, letter-spaced) indicating flower longevity, stem count, or handcrafted status.

### Lists
- Separated not by heavy lines, but by delicate blush gradients fading at the margins (`border-b: 1px solid rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.1)`).
- List icons utilize tiny floral florets or soft circular bullet nodes tinted in `#EC4899`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radios:** Pill-concentric circles. Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(120, 89, 102, 0.2)`. Checked: deep rose boundary with a soft blush inner disc.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (`8px` radius) transitioning to velvet rose when active, with clean white organic check indicators.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed in semi-translucent pill or generous oval shapes (`rounded-2xl`).
- Background: `rgba(255, 253, 251, 0.6)` with a delicate outer border (`1px solid rgba(120, 89, 102, 0.15)`).
- Focus state: Outline lifts with `#EC4899` hairline border and a soft `3px` aura (`rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.15)`). Placeholder styled in muted plum taupe.

### Cards
- **Product & Story Cards:** Frosted backdrop layers with `rounded-xl` (`3rem`) radius. Image frames inside cards carry `2rem` radii to create nested visual framing.
- Subtle inner borders with ambient rose drop shadows on hover create tactile "lifted gift box" depth.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Stem & Ribbon Customizer:** Segmented step-control wrapped in a single pill frame, allowing users to choose stem counts, wrapping paper textures, and velvet ribbon colors with live preview swatches.
- **Everlasting Care Accordion:** Soft translucent expandable panels detailing pipe cleaner preservation (dusting, reshaping, water avoidance) accented with sage mint callouts.
