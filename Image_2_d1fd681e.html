Surani's Flower Nook

The Maker's Story

Crafted with Quiet Love & Gentle Hands

Surani Blooms began with a tender idea: why should our deepest expressions of appreciation wilt in water after 72 hours? In our Colombo home studio, Surani began experimenting with high-density velvety chenille stems—discovering how wire tension, looping techniques, and color layering could mimic delicate rose petals, cheerful sunflowers, and serene lavender.

Today, every single flower bouquet carries hours of devoted craft. We do not mass-produce; every stem is twisted, assembled, and packed by Surani herself for graduation days, proposals, hospital cheer, and treasured anniversaries across Sri Lanka.

Care & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about caring for pipe cleaner flowers and delivery across the island.

How do I clean and care for my chenille flowers? expand_more
Chenille flowers are extremely low-maintenance! If dust settles over months, simply use a soft blush makeup brush or give them a gentle blow with a hairdryer on the cool shot setting from 30cm away. Keep them away from direct rain or high moisture.
Can I customize the color scheme and flower variety? expand_more
Yes! We specialize in bespoke orders. You can mix roses with sunflowers, change wrap colors to suit school graduation themes or wedding posy palettes. Use our online builder above or send us a WhatsApp photo reference.
How long does delivery take within Sri Lanka? expand_more
Ready-stock bouquets are dispatched within 24 hours. Custom multi-stem orders require 2–3 days for Surani to sculpt and assemble. We courier via tracked registered services: Colombo arrives next day; Outstation takes 2–3 days.
How are the bouquets packed for courier transit? expand_more
Every bouquet is placed in protective bubble wrap inside a sturdy, custom-sized rigid cardboard delivery carton to preserve the fluffy curls and organza wrapping without crushing.
